Sonic Scout third-party components

7-Zip: Copyright Igor Pavlov. Unmodified 7z.exe and 7z.dll are included for
extracting vendor packages without launching their interactive installers.
7-Zip is licensed under the GNU LGPL, with additional BSD/unRAR terms detailed
in tools/archive/License.txt. Source: https://www.7-zip.org/ and
https://github.com/ip7z/7zip. No 7-Zip installation is required on the tester PC.

VB-CABLE / Hi-Fi Cable: VB-Audio Software donationware. Driver packages and
their original notices are retained. https://vb-audio.com/Cable/

Equalizer APO and HeSuVi: GPL software. Original packages retain their notices.
https://sourceforge.net/projects/equalizerapo/
https://sourceforge.net/projects/hesuvi/

ReaPlugs: Copyright Cockos Incorporated. Not bundled or redistributed.
Setup downloads the original installer directly from https://www.reaper.fm/reaplugs/
and extracts it locally. Installation/use is subject to its vendor license,
retained as reaplugs_readme.txt. https://www.reaper.fm/reaplugs/

AutoEq: https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq
Selecting a game target requests headphone measurement compensation from
https://autoeq.app/ using the selected public headphone model and bundled game
target. No account, audio recording, diagnostics or other personal files are sent.
Generated EQ is saved locally and plays without an internet connection.
