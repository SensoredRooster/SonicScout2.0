BO7 V5 - 16 Channel Configs

This is a 16-channel profile. Pick ONE config file below and point a single
E-APO 'Include:' line at it. Do NOT add a HeSuVi include for 16ch.
The 16-channel endpoint does not expose Loudness Equalization, so
there is no LEQ setting to make and no LEQ note in this folder.

Recommended starting file: BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-UltraLoGun.txt

Classic+ [DEFAULT] [NEW] - Classic with more footstep lift. The surround and object ceilings are raised so distant steps come through louder, and the boost limiter is relaxed so close steps are no longer held back entirely.
  (Self Gun Level)
    Stock     BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-StockGun.txt
              Your gun untouched, exactly as the game mixes it.
    Low       BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-LoGun.txt
              Your own gunfire tamed the hardest, so shots never bury a footstep. NOTE: Low cuts self gun but reflections from the environment can still be loud, not tunable.
    Med       BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-MedGun.txt
              Your gun pulled back to sit behind the intel. The middle setting.
    High      BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-HiGun.txt
              A light trim on your own gunfire. Keeps most of its punch.
    Ultra Low BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-UltraLoGun.txt  (default)
              Your gun pushed to the engine's limit, 6 dB deeper than Low. NOTE: the room reflection after a shot is not ducked at this tier, so with the gun this far down the ring-out can become the loudest thing left.
