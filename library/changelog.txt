## 2026.09-V5Styles

- BO7 V5: three new tuning styles, Classic, Classic+ and Open. What they
  share is much slower leveller timing, and that turns out to be the biggest
  single change in how V5 feels. The processing stops drawing attention to
  itself and the tune sits far closer to the old Loudness Equalization
  character, while keeping V5's footstep detail and gunfire control.
- Classic is the calmest of the three, and the least audible processing in
  the family. If V5 has ever felt like it was working on the sound, this is
  the one to try first.
- Classic+ is Classic with more footstep lift: distant steps come through
  louder, and close ones are no longer held back the way they were. File
  names spell it ClassicPlus.
- Open is the least filtered of the three, built on Balanced's more forward
  voicing rather than Competitive's, so mid-level sound comes up harder and
  less bright content is held back.
- New self gun level on every style: Ultra Low (UltraLoGun). It sits below
  Low and pushes your own gunfire to the engine's limit. One thing to listen
  for: the room reflection after a shot is not ducked at this tier, so with
  the gun this far down the ring-out can end up the loudest part of your own
  fire. If that bothers you, Low is unchanged and one file away.
- Recommended starting file: BO7_V5_16ch_ClassicPlus-UltraLoGun.txt.
- Your own footsteps are held down harder and for longer, on every style and
  every self gun level. The dip now covers the whole of your footfall rather
  than only its leading edge, holding for around two thirds of a second, and
  it goes deeper, so your own steps stay clear of the ones you are listening
  for.
- Your own gunfire comes back up more gradually to match, on every self gun
  level except Stock: roughly six to eight tenths of a second after firing
  rather than about a third.
- Capped, Immersive and Balanced are deprecated. They are the original V5
  styles, and the newer ones cover the same ground better. Nothing is
  removed: their files still ship and still work.
- Full and Competitive are unchanged apart from the footstep change above.
- V5 now ships 40 configs: eight styles at five self gun levels.
- The V5 target curve is unchanged, so your existing V5 squig.link EQ still
  applies.
- New headphone measurements: the Tipsy TG4, in both switch positions, and
  the I/O Audio Cielo.
- All other games and versions are unchanged.

## 2026.07.1-Overhaul

- BO7 V5: Full and Balanced retuned. Full now lifts footsteps with combat
  ducking off; Balanced keeps Full's footstep lift with combat control on,
  sitting between Full and Competitive.
- BO7 V5 target curve replaced: log-spaced points in place of the old
  linear 2 Hz grid, with revised gains. Re-run your squig.link EQ against
  the new target if you use V5.
- Capped, Competitive and Immersive are unchanged.
- All other games and versions are unchanged.

## 2026.07-Overhaul

- Library restructured: catalog-driven layout (catalog.json, game-configs,
  Targets, hesuvi, vst, equalizer). Season folders retired.
- Naming: seasons (S#) become versions (V#) across all games. Tunes are
  not tied to game seasons and the names no longer pretend they are.
- BO7 V4 added: native SS Spatial Engine Bravo (8ch), EAC Refined HRIR
  included at both sample rates.
- BO7 V5 added: 16-channel spatial audio. 5 tunes x 4 self-gun levels
  (20 configs). Binaural rendering happens in-plugin; no HeSuVi stage.
- SS Spatial Engine Bravo VST binaries included (v1.0.2, v2.0.0).
  Proprietary, binary-only. See vst/LICENSE.
- TargetOnly EQ now uses a flat target for all games (tuning logic
  change). Per-game TargetOnly files retired.
- STD flat baseline profile added.

## 2026.06.95-V

- BO7 Version 5 tuning styles have new names: Full (the standard tune, now the
  default), Capped (Full with a soft peak ceiling), Immersive (the easy listen),
  Balanced (the middle ground), and Competitive (maximum footstep detail).
- Every V5 style now has a Self Gun Level: Low, Med, or High. Low keeps your own
  gunfire quietest and is the default; High keeps more of your own gun in the mix
  while still taming it. Switch levels from the dashboard, right beside the
  tuning style.
- Hover any tuning style in the picker to see what it sounds like and who it's for.
- Existing V5 profiles fall back to the default style (Full) after this update -
  re-pick your style once if you were on a different one.
- The Self Gun Level picker and style descriptions need app 1.2.95. Older app
  versions keep working and load every style at its Low gun level.

## 2026.06.91-V

- BO7 Version 5 now includes a selectable "V2" tuning variation (retuned 16ch SS Spatial
  Engine config). The original V5 tuning stays the default - pick "V2" in the variation menu.
- LANDMARK: BO7 Version 5 - the first 16-channel release. The SS Spatial Engine v2.0.0
  runs the entire chain natively on the 16-channel SonicScout2.0 + path: one engine end to
  end, replacing the old multi-stage stack.
- V5 sounds close to stock - clean and natural - with enemy footsteps boosted and
  everything that fights them pulled out of the way. Your own gunfire and your own
  footsteps stay low, and 16 discrete channels give every sound its own place:
  separation the 8-channel path can't reach.
- Windows Loudness Equalization is gone from the chain entirely. The engine handles
  loudness itself now - in parts, per channel and per band, and much faster - so a
  loud event in one place no longer drags down a quiet footstep somewhere else. Less
  pumping, less smear, more of the game's real dynamics.
- Seasons are now Versions: S3 is now V3, S4 is now V4, and so on across the entire
  library. Your existing profiles migrate automatically on update - nothing to remake,
  nothing to rename.
- To try V5: create a new BO7 V5 profile. No HRIR pick, no channel selector - the app
  configures itself. V5 requires the SonicScout2.0 + output; if activation says it isn't
  configured, re-run Setup once.
- V4 and every earlier version keep working exactly as before on the 8-channel path.
- Beta note: library updates during beta may reset custom or imported EQ files - back
  yours up before updating. A permanent fix is in the pipeline.
